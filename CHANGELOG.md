# Changelog

## 1.0

First release.

- Recipe names in the profession window's list are colored by skill-up chance: orange, yellow or green, matching the arrows next to them.
- The "[2]" craftable count after a name gets the same color.
- The recipe name on the right side of the window uses the same color instead of its item rarity.
- Recipes that give no more skill-ups keep the game's normal color (optional gray via `COLOR_GRAY` at the top of the .lua).
